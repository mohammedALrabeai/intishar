# Market Size & Competitive Landscape: White-Label Mobile Shopping Apps for Zid & Salla Merchants (KSA)

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. Currency: SAR, pegged at 3.75 SAR = 1 USD. "Official" means company-published or company-attributed. "Estimate" means a third-party model or tracker. App-market data comes from the public APIs behind apps.zid.sa (`https://api.zid.sa/v1/market/public/...`) and apps.salla.sa (`https://api.salla.dev/marketplace/v2/...`), pulled on 10 Nov 2026. Zid app prices appear to include VAT: 3,300.01 = 2,869.57 + 15%.

## 1. Zid and Salla: merchants, GMV, funding, growth, and share of mid/large merchants

### Takeaway
Zid is about one-fifth of Salla's size. It reports roughly 12–13.5k merchants against Salla's 65–80k, and about SAR 10bn of cumulative GMV against Salla's SAR 45bn+. Zid's last disclosed round is a USD 50M Series B from 2021/22. Salla raised a USD 130M pre-IPO round in 2024. Neither platform publishes how many of its merchants are mid-sized or large, so the pool of buyers for a 3,000–18,000 SAR/yr app has to be estimated from proxies.

### Cited Findings
**Zid (official or company-attributed)**
- More than 12,000 active merchants in 2024. The merchant base grew more than 30% in 2024, merchant GMV grew 25% YoY, basket size and conversion each rose about 50%, and the platform holds over 4M SKUs. Total funding is USD 59M, including a USD 50M Series B in 2021. Article dated 30 Dec 2024 — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2584723/business-economy)
- The Zid homepage, undated and read Nov 2026, claims 13,500+ merchants, more than SAR 10bn in sales through Zid stores (cumulative), 15M+ shoppers, 400+ apps, and 7 markets (KSA, UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Egypt, Syria) — [zid.sa](https://zid.sa/en/)
- The public API listed 536 apps in the Zid App Market on 10 Nov 2026 — [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/)
- Zid says 71%+ of its stores rely on apps for daily operations, and that partner apps average 72% growth in activations within 12 months. These are company claims, seen in a search snippet of Zid's partner pages — [Zid partners solutions](https://zid.sa/en/partners-solutions)
- Zid's Retail Sector Report 2025 is based on more than 30M orders. It reports +28% growth in one unnamed sector, and 4 sectors account for 72% of demand. No GMV total is given — [Zid Retail Sector Report 2025](https://zid.sa/en/reports/retail-sector/)
- Sources disagree on the Series B date: Arab News says 2021, while some trackers say 2022 and name IMPACT46 as lead. Total funding is put at USD 57–59M. I found no 2025–2026 round — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/zid/__xu1Ob2DOOlXwEwgRQLgpw-FPMxdhxOF5SB-4savnhiA); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2584723/business-economy)
- Zid's plans: Starter is free, Rise is SAR 99/mo, Growth is SAR 299/mo with 5 seats, and Professional is "Let's talk" (custom) with 20 seats. Every plan includes a "Mobile & Tablet POS App". No plan includes a branded shopper app. Pricing page read Nov 2026 — [Zid pricing](https://zid.sa/en/pricing/)

**Zid (third-party estimates)**
- StoreLeads tracks Zid stores on custom domains: 8,396 live in 2024 Q4, 9,014 in 2025 Q4, and 10,111 in 2026 Q3. It shows 14,624 "live" as of Nov 2026, but that partial 2026 Q4 jump is unexplained. 89.9% of stores are in Saudi Arabia. Top categories are Apparel (15.5%), Beauty & Fitness (9.5%) and Home & Garden (7.5%). By product count: 3,485 stores carry 1–9 products, 277 carry 100–249, 152 carry 250–999, and about 40 carry 1,000+ — [StoreLeads Zid report](https://storeleads.app/reports/zid)
- An unsourced market report claims SAR 1.6bn in annual sales by Zid merchants. Treat it as unverified — summarized in [search results referencing task.com.sa](https://task.com.sa/en/blog/saudi-ecommerce-platforms-salla-zid-2016-2025)

**Salla (official or company-attributed)**
- Raised USD 130M in a 2024 pre-IPO round led by Investcorp, with Sanabil (a PIF company) and STV. Reports 80,000+ active merchants across the region, over USD 7bn in cumulative sales enabled since 2020, and 400+ ecosystem apps. Dated 30 Dec 2024 — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2584723/business-economy)
- In an Al Arabiya interview on 9 Mar 2025, CEO Nawaf Hariri reportedly said 66,000 merchants used Salla in 2024, about SAR 14bn of GMV went through the platform that year, and cumulative GMV since launch was SAR 41bn. He also said Salla aims to list within about 3 years. This comes from a search-result summary because the page returned 403 — [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/videos/market-pulse/2025/03/09/-%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A9-66-%D8%A3%D9%84%D9%81-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A)
- A Salla blog post from about Sep 2026 claims "more than 65,000 active subscribed stores" and cumulative sales above SAR 45bn — [Salla blog (Nml investment)](https://salla.com/blog/nml)
- Crunchbase says 68,000+ active merchants, 15M+ consumers, and USD 13.3bn of cumulative GMV processed — [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/salla)
- The Salla Partners site says developers can reach "60,000+ merchants" — [Salla Partners](https://salla.partners/apps)
- Tracxn lists USD 138M raised over 3 rounds. Dealroom estimates the 2025 valuation at USD 1–2.5bn — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/salla/__5ZaGmWjJdmZ75SIbfQVg06ywmpoqB0paTyg7E9Y06Qg); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/salla/)
- StoreLeads' Salla data looks broken. Quarterly counts fall from 14,326 in 2024 Q4 to 184 in 2025 Q1, and only 28.4% of stores are geo-tagged to KSA. Many Salla stores probably run on salla.sa subdomains that the tracker misses, so do not use this source for Salla — [StoreLeads Salla report](https://storeleads.app/reports/salla)

### Inferences
- **Relative scale.** Zid has about 17–20% of Salla's merchant count (13.5k vs 65–80k) and about 22% of its cumulative GMV (SAR 10bn vs 45bn). Salla's 2024 GMV was about SAR 14bn. If Zid's annual GMV is in proportion, it is likely around SAR 2.5–3.5bn/yr. This is an estimate, not a disclosed figure.
- **Mid/large proxy.** Zid's paid tiers run up to Growth at SAR 3,588/yr, with Professional priced on request. An app costing SAR 3,000–18,000/yr equals 1–5x a merchant's entire platform subscription, so realistic buyers are Growth or Professional merchants with meaningful repeat-purchase volume.
- **Product-count proxy.** StoreLeads finds about 470 Zid stores with 100+ products, around 3% of tracked stores. This is a weak but consistent signal that the serious mid/large segment is in the hundreds to low thousands, not the tens of thousands.
- **Funding gap.** Zid has not announced a round since about 2021/22, while Salla raised a pre-IPO round in 2024. That gap may affect Zid's ability to build a first-party app builder; see the risks section.

### Gaps
- Neither company publishes an official 2025 or 2026 annual GMV.
- Neither company publishes a split of merchants by plan tier or revenue band, so the true count of mid/large merchants is unknown.
- I could not verify the original Al Arabiya transcript because the page returned 403.
- I found no Salla IPO prospectus, which would hold audited segment data.

## 2. Mobile commerce in Saudi Arabia: mobile share, apps vs mobile web, retention

### Takeaway
Smartphones carry about 78–79% of Saudi B2C e-commerce value (Mordor, 2025–2026), and nearly all internet use is mobile. I found no credible source that splits native-app purchases from mobile-web purchases for KSA overall. Shopping-app retention is weak globally, roughly 2–5% at Day 30, which argues for push and loyalty features over raw download counts.

### Cited Findings
- Mordor (updated 23 Oct 2026): Saudi e-commerce is USD 31.29bn in 2026 and reaches USD 60.7bn by 2032, an 11.63% CAGR over 2027–2032. Smartphones hold 78.79% of B2C e-commerce in 2026 and are the fastest-growing device at a 12.77% CAGR. Cards are 41.84% of B2C payments, and mobile wallets are the fastest-growing method. — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-market)
- An earlier Mordor snapshot, seen in a search snippet, gave USD 27.96bn for 2025, with smartphones at 77.98% of B2C revenue — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-market)
- Market-size estimates disagree widely: from USD 16.3bn (P&S) to USD 251.3bn (IMARC) for 2025. A separate B2C databook puts 2024 at USD 18.78bn and 2029 at USD 28.8bn — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-e-commerce-market); [P&S](https://www.psmarketresearch.com/market-analysis/saudi-arabia-e-commerce-market-report); [Yahoo/ResearchAndMarkets](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/saudi-arabia-b2c-ecommerce-business-102700788.html)
- Mordor also cites Jarir's revamped app (2025): a 4.8 store rating, doubled customer conversion, and 60% more downloads — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-market)
- Visa/PYMNTS 2024 Global Digital Shopping Index, Saudi edition (Oct 2024; N=1,076 consumers, fielded 27 Sep–1 Dec 2023): 72.2% of Saudi consumers want "a mobile app or mobile-specific site" from merchants (74.8% in another segment). 77.2% want their preferred payment method, and 72.9% want an easy store and cart. The report tells merchants to provide "dedicated mobile apps" — [Visa Acceptance PDF](https://www.visaacceptance.com/content/dam/documents/campaign/shopping-index/global-digital-shopping-index-2024-saudi-arabia-edition.pdf)
- A secondary press release (28 Sep 2026) cites CST data: 76.9% of internet users shopped online in 2025, and mobile phones accounted for 99.6% of internet usage — [express-press-release](https://express-press-release.net/news/2026/09/28/1772213)
- P&S estimates that apps account for about 75% of Saudi online grocery sales in 2025. This is a grocery-only model estimate — [P&S Intelligence](https://www.psmarketresearch.com/market-analysis/saudi-arabia-online-grocery-market-report)
- Low-quality, unsourced blog claims: app conversion of 3–5% vs 1–2% on mobile browsers, and more than 80% of purchases on mobile. Do not use these as evidence — [Watily](https://watily.com/blog/en/mobile-app-vs-browser-repeat-customers-saudi-2026/)
- Retention: Adjust's regional data (Apr 2024) puts MENA retention across all app categories at Day 1 24%, Day 7 10%, Day 30 4%, with MENA showing the steepest drop by Day 30 — [Adjust blog](https://www.adjust.com/blog/what-makes-a-good-retention-rate/)
- Secondary 2026 roundups relay Adjust 2025 shopping-app retention of about 12% on Day 1 and about 2% on Day 30, with e-commerce overall at about 3% on Day 30. An older AppsFlyer figure is 4.83% and an older Statista figure is 5.6%. I did not verify these against the primary reports — [UXCam roundup](https://uxcam.com/blog/mobile-app-retention-benchmarks/); [Panto roundup](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/mobile-app-retention-statistics)

### Inferences
- With about 78% of value already on smartphones, the pitch for a native app is not "go mobile". It is better repeat purchasing: push, loyalty, and app-only offers. That fits mid/large merchants with repeat-purchase categories such as apparel, beauty and food, which are also Zid's largest categories.
- Day-30 retention of about 2–4% means small merchants with little traffic will see few active app users. This supports targeting larger merchants and selling on push-driven re-engagement.

### Gaps
- I found no KSA-wide, credible split of native-app vs mobile-web sales from Checkout.com, Mastercard, CST, MCIT or the e-commerce council.
- I found no KSA- or GCC-specific shopping-app retention benchmark from Adjust or AppsFlyer.
- I did not obtain the original CST Internet Report.

## 3. Competitors serving Zid merchants with mobile apps, and whether Zid has a first-party app

### Takeaway
Zid has no first-party branded shopper-app builder. Instead, its own dashboard (Channels → Mobile App) routes merchants to Anakeed (عناقيد التطبيقات), which Zid's help center calls "Zid's partner". The Zid App Market has a dedicated subcategory, "تطبيقات الجوال للمتجر" (store mobile apps), with exactly 6 apps. Anakeed dominates by visible adoption, with 56 ratings against 19 for the other five combined. List prices run from about SAR 2,156 to 16,000 per year.

### Cited Findings
- **Zid's channel integration.** In the Zid dashboard, Channels → "Mobile App" opens the Anakeed listing. Anakeed is described as "شريكة زد" (Zid's partner) and builds and publishes the app. The help page lists package names Basic, Advanced, Quick Track and Professional; abandoned-cart alerts need Professional. Development takes 10–20 working days, plus 2–14 days of store review. Anakeed can publish under its own Apple sub-account for free and transfer the app later. A Zid dashboard card shows app-only orders, sales, average order value and discounts. Page updated 2 Nov 2026 — [Zid Help Center: تطبيق متجرك على الجوال](https://help.zid.sa/ar/mobile-app)
- **Zid's own apps are merchant tools, not shopper apps.** "زد – متجرك في جوالك" is a store-management app for merchants on iOS and Android, and Zid also offers a POS app — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zid-your-store-on-your-phone/id6443907047); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sa.zid.merchants&hl=en_US); [Zid pricing](https://zid.sa/en/pricing/)
- **The mobile-app subcategory.** Zid's categories API lists subcategory id 66, "تطبيقات الجوال للمتجر", under "Store & Customer Experience", with application_count = 6 as of 10 Nov 2026 — [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/) (API: `api.zid.sa/v1/market/public/categories`)

### Inferences
- **Anakeed's position.** Anakeed holds a structural advantage: default placement in Zid's dashboard, help-center documentation, the "Zid partner" label, and about 75% of all ratings in the category (56 of 75). A new entrant would compete as an alternative listing in a 6-app subcategory, not as the default channel.
- **Low adoption across the category.** Five of the six listings have 12 or fewer ratings, and two have none. Either total demand is thin or the default placement absorbs most of it. Ratings understate installs, but the order of magnitude is likely in the low hundreds of active Zid apps.
- **Price bands.** The market clusters around three bands:
  - Entry: SAR 2,150–3,350/yr (MT Themes, Tajadad, Splendapp, Tatbiqi, Anakeed Basic).
  - Mid: SAR 5,000–6,000/yr (Anakeed Advanced, Tajadad Growth, Neoxero Basic).
  - Premium: SAR 9,000–16,000/yr (Neoxero Custom, Anakeed Ultra).
  "1 year + 1 free" offers on Anakeed and Neoxero cut the effective annual price roughly in half.

### Gaps
- Install counts are not exposed by the Zid market API.
- Ratings have no text, so there is no complaint or churn signal.
- Anakeed's revenue-share terms with Zid, and any exclusivity, are not public.
- The help page's package names (Quick Track, Professional) differ from the market's current plans (Explore, Basic, Advanced, Ultra), so the help page may be out of date.

## 4. Competitors serving Salla merchants with mobile apps

### Takeaway
Salla has taken this market for itself. Its first-party "صانع التطبيقات" (App Maker) costs SAR 550/month or 5,550/yr, with 450+ apps launched. A full crawl of the Salla App Store (851 apps) found no third-party shopper-app builders. The App Store's "App Maker" category holds only Salla-published add-ons for its own builder. Third-party options exist only outside the store, through agencies and freelancers.

### Cited Findings
- **Salla App Maker landing page** (dated Nov 2026): native iOS and Android, drag-and-drop design, unlimited push (instant, scheduled and automated, including abandoned cart and back-in-stock), app sales analytics, and integrations with Zendesk, Intercom, Firebase, OneSignal, Adjust and Smartlook. It claims 450+ apps launched with App Maker, a 45% average increase in purchase frequency, and up to 85% of sales via the app for some merchants. Price shows as SAR 550/mo or 5,500/yr, but the FAQ on the same page says 500/mo, so the page contradicts itself. Requires a Plus or Pro plan. Salla can create developer accounts for SAR 990 — [Salla App Maker](https://salla.com/app-maker)
- **Salla help article** (updated 22 Aug 2026, but flagged "old article"): SAR 550/mo or 5,550/yr including VAT. Merchants without developer accounts pay an extra SAR 990 setup fee, for 6,490 in year one. Apple ($99/yr) and Google ($25) fees are extra. The merchant needs a paid Plus or Pro plan, and the Special plan includes App Maker. Other requirements: a custom domain, company-type developer accounts, and a DUNS number. Publishing takes about 72 hours with existing accounts, or 2 weeks to 1 month otherwise — [Salla Help: الاشتراك في صانع التطبيقات](https://help.salla.sa/article/531264824)
- **Salla App Store crawl** (public marketplace API, 10 Nov 2026): 851 apps across 18 categories. Category "صانع التطبيقات" (id 488637663) holds 6 apps, all published by Salla as add-ons to its App Maker:
  - Adjust: no ratings.
  - OneSignal: 4.6★, 9 ratings.
  - Smartlook: 4.1★, 16 ratings.
  - Intercom: 5★, 18 ratings.
  - Store Blog: 4.6★, 946 ratings.
  - Zendesk: 5★, 3 ratings.

  A keyword search across all 851 apps (mobile app, iOS, Android, App Store, PWA, native) found no shopper-app builders, only merchant tools such as Tayyar driver management and Marqam analytics — [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa/ar)
- **Private apps.** Partners can build "private apps" for individual stores through the Salla Partners portal, with ID verification and store-owner approval. These are not listed publicly — [Salla Help: private apps](https://help.salla.sa/article/%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%87%D8%A7/vhx4rqqiz2b4ilfihkarz42g)
- **Off-store agencies and freelancers that serve Salla merchants:**
  - A "Smile shop" listing sells Salla-store-to-app conversion for SAR 2,500, including store fees, business email and domain, but excluding the Salla subscription. It requires a custom domain — [simpershop](https://simpershop.com/RAjdzBz)
  - Konooz advertises store-to-app conversion with no public price — [konoozag.com](https://konoozag.com/%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82/)
  - Banafsijy offers e-commerce app development — [banafsijy.com](https://banafsijy.com/product/e-commerce-mobile-apps/)
  - Freelance requests to convert a Salla store into an iOS and Android app appear on Nafezly — [Nafezly](https://nafezly.com/project/20037-%D9%85%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A8-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%81%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AF)
- Other Saudi players appeared in search but I could not confirm Salla or Zid support. Matajer (mapp.sa) is a separate commerce platform with native apps in its Pro plan. Tajir App (tajirapp.com) offers a custom app with the store's icon — [mapp.sa](https://mapp.sa/); [tajirapp.com](https://tajirapp.com/)

### Inferences
- **Salla is the precedent for platform risk.** Salla built the shopper-app builder itself, bundled it into its top plan, and left no third-party builder in its store. A new "Anakeed-like" product cannot realistically enter Salla through the official marketplace.
- **Penetration benchmark.** 450+ App Maker apps across about 65–80k Salla merchants is roughly 0.6–0.7%, even with a first-party, well-promoted product. If all 450 apps are active and annual, Salla's App Maker run-rate is at most about SAR 2.5M/yr. Applying that penetration to Zid's ~13.5k merchants gives about 80–100 apps. That is consistent with the ~75 ratings observed across Zid's six builders.
- **Price anchor.** Salla's SAR 5,550/yr anchors local pricing. Zid's mid band (Anakeed Advanced at 5,500, Neoxero at 6,000, Tajadad Growth at 5,000) sits at the same price.

### Gaps
- No public data on how many of Salla's 450+ apps are still active, or on churn.
- I could not determine whether Salla's partner terms forbid third-party shopper-app builders, or whether there are simply none listed.
- Agency pricing and volumes are unverified.

## 5. Regional and global players active in the GCC: do any support Zid or Salla?

### Takeaway
I found no evidence that Mobikul, MageNative, Shopney, Plobal, Tapcart, Vajro/Superfans or Appy Pie integrate with Zid or Salla. None appear in either app market. In practice, competition on Zid is limited to the 6 local listings plus off-market agencies and generic web-to-app wrappers.

### Cited Findings
- I checked the full Zid catalog (536 apps) and Salla catalog (851 apps) on 10 Nov 2026 for Mobikul, MageNative, Shopney, Tapcart, Vajro, Plobal, Appy Pie, AppMySite, Twinr, Median/GoNative and Webkul. None appear on Zid. On Salla, Webkul appears only as an Odoo connector — [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/); [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa/ar)
- Mobikul (Webkul) and MageNative target Magento and Shopify. Listings put Mobikul from about $25/mo and MageNative from about $20/mo, with a 14-day trial. I found no Salla or Zid support — [ecommercetech.io Mobikul](https://ecommercetech.io/apps/mobikul); [Webkul Mobikul](https://mobikul-sh.webkul.com/)
- The global Shopify-centric builders (Tapcart, Shopney, Plobal, Vajro) are covered in Shopify comparisons with no mention of Salla or Zid — [Digismoothie](https://www.digismoothie.com/best-shopify-apps/mobile-app); [Appbrew](https://www.appbrew.com/blogs/best-shopney-alternatives-for-shopify-stores)
- Anakeed is Saudi, with phone number +966 53 233 1339 and email Apps@AppsBunches.com per its Zid developer record. Splendapp, MT Themes, Tatbiqi, Neoxero and Tajadad are regional developers on Zid. Neoxero's site is a .net domain and the others use .com or .app; I did not research their countries of origin — [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/)

### Inferences
- **Why global players are absent.** Zid's custom APIs, Arabic RTL and theme compatibility (Zid themes plus SDK themes such as Al-Asayel, Farah, Ibraq and Ronq, per the Zid help page), together with SAR invoicing, form a moat against global players. The moat also means a new local entrant must bear the same integration cost.
- **Wrapper risk.** Generic web-to-app wrappers such as Median or AppMySite could wrap any Zid storefront as a webview app. They are a low-end substitute risk that I did not research.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether any Egyptian or Jordanian agency publicly markets Zid-specific apps outside the market.
- I did not check pricing or Zid compatibility for generic wrappers.

## 8. Key risks: platform risk, API terms, and Zid vs Salla concentration

### Takeaway
The biggest risks are (a) Zid's default routing of its "Mobile App" channel to Anakeed, (b) the Salla precedent of a platform taking app building in-house, (c) a revenue share with Zid set by private agreement, and (d) a small Zid base, about one-fifth of Salla's, of which only a few hundred to a few thousand merchants are realistic buyers.

### Cited Findings
- **Partner lock-in.** Zid's help center embeds Anakeed as the partner behind Channels → Mobile App, with instructions to log into "the Anakeed dashboard" using the Zid store email. Updated 2 Nov 2026 — [Zid Help Center](https://help.zid.sa/ar/mobile-app)
- **First-party precedent.** Salla runs its own App Maker, bundles it into the Special plan, and lists only its own add-ons in the App Maker category. No third-party builders were found among 851 apps — [Salla Help](https://help.salla.sa/article/531264824); [Salla App Store](https://apps.salla.sa/ar)
- **Zid partner terms.** Zid charges nothing to list, but "the distribution of revenue between Zid and you will be determined and formalized through a partnership agreement". Apps go through review and an official agreement before publishing. Separately, the channel or affiliate program pays up to 35% commission on subscriptions. Source: search snippet of Zid partner pages — [Zid partners solutions](https://zid.sa/en/partners-solutions); [developers.zid.sa](https://developers.zid.sa)
- **Theme dependency.** Neoxero markets itself as "the first mobile app compatible with Zid's new theme system". Anakeed's help-page compatibility list names Zid themes and SDK themes. Each Zid storefront or theme change forces builders to re-integrate — [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/); [Zid Help Center](https://help.zid.sa/ar/mobile-app)
- **Zid investing in its marketplace.** Zid has advertised a "Product Manager – App Marketplace & Developer Experience" role. Zid also lists a first-party "Zid AI" developer entry (ai.zid.dev) in its market, so it builds some apps itself — [job listing](https://jobs.global.vc/companies/zid-2/jobs/46305042-product-manager-app-marketplace-developer-experience); [Zid App Market](https://apps.zid.sa/ar-sa/)
- **Scale and capital gap.** Zid has about 13.5k merchants and SAR 10bn+ cumulative GMV, with its last disclosed round in 2021/22. Salla has 65–80k merchants, SAR 45bn+ cumulative GMV, and a 2024 pre-IPO raise — [zid.sa](https://zid.sa/en/); [Salla blog](https://salla.com/blog/nml); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2584723/business-economy)
- **App-store friction.** Builders and merchants need company developer accounts, DUNS numbers, Apple's $99/yr fee, and 2–14 days of review — [Zid Help Center](https://help.zid.sa/ar/mobile-app); [Salla Help](https://help.salla.sa/article/531264824)

### Inferences
- **Zid platform risk is real but not imminent.** Instead of building, Zid has chosen to partner by embedding Anakeed. It could still copy Salla, or acquire or extend the Anakeed partnership into exclusivity. A new entrant should assume no dashboard placement and limited marketing support from Zid.
- **Rough market sizing for Zid** (inference with explicit assumptions):
  - Zid has about 13,500 merchants.
  - At Salla-like penetration of 0.7%, that is about 95 apps; at 3%, about 400; at 5%, about 675.
  - At a blended about SAR 4,500/yr (a mix of the 3.3k and 5.5k tiers, net of "1+1" offers), annual market revenue would be about SAR 0.4M, 1.8M or 3.0M respectively, shared among 6+ vendors.
  - Adding Salla through off-market or private apps is speculative, given Salla's first-party product.
  - GCC expansion via Zid adds little: about 90% of Zid stores are in KSA (StoreLeads).
- **Viable positions.** On these numbers, a pure Zid-only "Anakeed clone" faces a small, partner-dominated pool. Viable angles are:
  - multi-platform support (Zid, Salla private apps, Shopify-KSA, WooCommerce);
  - performance or revenue-share pricing;
  - clearly differentiated ROI features (loyalty, cart recovery, segmentation, AI merchandising);
  - a low-cost entry tier below SAR 2,500/yr to compete with MT Themes and Tajadad.

### Gaps
- I did not find the actual Zid revenue-share percentage for app developers, any exclusivity clause, or API rate-limit terms.
- I found no public statement from Zid about plans for a first-party shopper-app builder.
- Not researched: Apple App Store Guideline 4.2/4.3 enforcement against template-built apps, which is a known risk for white-label builders.
